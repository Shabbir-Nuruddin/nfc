# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + Tailwind CSS. Confirmed by the user on 2026-10-02. The site lives in `10_Website/site/`.

## Users

- Primary: Dawoodi Bohra families and individuals who recite a nightly or morning dua and want the recitation one tap away at the bedside, without opening YouTube and searching.
- Secondary: buyers gifting the object (weddings, misaaq, Ramadan, new homes, elders).
- Tag users are often on a phone, in low light, about to sleep. The player page must work half-asleep, one-handed, in the dark.

## Product Purpose

A physical object with an NFC chip sealed inside. Tapping it with a phone opens a web page that plays the chosen dua immediately, with the text alongside. Success means repeat nightly taps, not first-tap novelty.

## Positioning

Designed from the Fatimi visual heritage the community already recognises (al-Aqmar and al-Anwar in Cairo, restored by the Dawoodi Bohras; Fatimi gateways echoed at Saifee Masjid), not generic "Islamic geometry". No app, no account, no battery.

## Operating Context

- Bedside table, lamp, hanging from a bedpost or lamp, beside a dua book.
- Phone tap reads NFC (iPhone XS and later read in the background; Android needs NFC on and the screen unlocked).
- Browsers block autoplay with sound in many cases; the player must degrade to a single large "begin" control.
