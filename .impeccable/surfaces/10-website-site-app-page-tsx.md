# Surface brief: NFC Dua product site + hidden tap player

Scope: `/` product and pre-order page (Persuade). Related: `/d/[slug]` tap player (Operate, unlinked, noindex).
Audience: Dawoodi Bohra households and gift buyers. Job: understand "tap and it plays", choose a colourway, pre-order on WhatsApp.
Constraints: no invented prices, testimonials, dua text or recitations. Dua text and audio are data slots the owner fills.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The tag is a tiraz band. Fatimid Egypt wove inscription bands into plain cloth; this page is built as woven bands of meaning across a dark ground, refusing the cream, brass and star-geometry gift-shop page.

OWN-WORLD: Night indigo ground (#0f1430 family), madder-red silk bands, saffron thread hairlines, kohl-black outlines. Tags render in their own colourways. Reem Kufi for display (Arabic and Latin, one constructed family), Schibsted Grotesk for text. Components are bands: full-width horizontal strips with woven 1px thread edges, never cards. Square corners on bands, keel-arch silhouette reserved for the object.

STORY: Visitors see the object, then watch a tap open the player. They see why the arch and medallion are Fatimi and Bohra-specific (Aqmar), pick a colourway, read the honest specs, then pre-order by WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: name and one-line thesis at 4.5rem, WhatsApp pre-order primary, "Watch the tap" secondary. Right, 55% width: live 3D keel-arch tag, rotatable, with a phone that taps it on click and blooms into the player preview. A madder tiraz band runs across the bottom edge.

FORM: Fatimid tiraz textile bands, position 3 of my ordered list (1 Aqmar night facade, 2 rock crystal, 3 tiraz, 4 lustreware, 5 Bab al-Futuh stone). Seed key ff270f9e.
Raise (from cyclorama, declined): discrete named phases, Night and Morning, labelled, colour never the only signal. The player and page close carry them.
Raise (from six-pack, declined): every control shows state as well as value (speed, position, loaded or missing media).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
